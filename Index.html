ABOUT

SODARRSZN is a hip-hop artist from Bindura, Zimbabwe, born 14 April 2003. He writes and records as Maulana Malvin Musa and releases his music through OUTTERSPACE.

Raised on Nas, Drake and Kanye West, he broke through in 2024 with DEARLY DEPARTED, MR PRESIDENT and the YOU STARTED, I'LL FINISH IT project — then went into overdrive in 2026 with two albums, ALL DOGS GO TO HEAVEN and THE MAIN BIRD, a run of singles, and the CEO-SODARR 1 EP.

I WAS NEVER LUCKY, YOU SHOULD'VE TAKEN ME SERIOUSLY is out now, with a documentary announced for December. Follow him on Instagram for updates.
